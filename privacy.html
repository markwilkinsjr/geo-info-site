Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Summary

GeoInfo sells printed route posters. We do not sell personal data. We access your Google account only for the narrow tasks that run our business — reading your email to answer you, sending email on your behalf, and keeping our own sales and production records in Google Sheets and Drive. We never use your data for advertising.

Who we are

GeoInfo is a product of Mark Wilkins Jr. ("GeoInfo", "we", "us"). Contact: markrwilkinsjr@gmail.com

Google account data we access

We connect to your Google account using OAuth. We request only the permissions listed below, and only when you authorise the connection.

  • Gmail (read, send, modify) — to read your questions and order requests, to send order confirmations and replies, and to label or archive our own correspondence so the mailbox stays organised.
  • Drive — to store and retrieve artwork files, order records, and production assets.
  • Sheets — to maintain sales, partner, and production tracking spreadsheets.
  • Documents — to create and edit order documents and specifications.
  • Calendar — to schedule production and shipping work.
  • Contacts (read-only) — to look up a customer or partner contact you have already shared with us.

How we use it

  • To answer your enquiries and take your order.
  • To send you order updates, shipping information, and replies.
  • To produce, print, and ship your poster.
  • To maintain our internal sales and partner records.
  • To detect and respond to abuse of the service.

We do not use your Google data for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or share your data with data brokers.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as needed to fulfil orders, handle disputes, and meet tax and accounting obligations. Correspondence is retained while it remains relevant to an order or enquiry, and archived afterwards for a reasonable period. You can ask us to delete anything we are not legally required to keep.

Who we share it with

We share only what is necessary to do the job:

  • Print and shipping suppliers — your delivery address and the artwork file needed to produce and ship your poster.
  • Payment processors — transaction details needed to take payment.
  • Professional advisers — accountants or legal advisers, where required.
  • Law enforcement or regulators — where we are legally obliged to disclose.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, for any purpose.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Google (Gmail, Drive, Sheets) and with our print and shipping suppliers. We do not operate our own servers.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct anything inaccurate.
  • Delete data we are not required to keep.
  • Stop us using your data for a particular purpose.

You can also revoke our access to your Google account at any time through your Google Account security settings, and manage your Google data directly through Google Takeout and your account's activity controls.

Security

We use Google's OAuth authorisation, which grants us scoped access without us ever seeing or storing your Google password. Access tokens are held securely and refreshed automatically. We use reputable print and shipping suppliers with their own operational security.

International transfers

Google and some of our suppliers process data outside your country of residence. Where that happens, the transfers are covered by the safeguards those providers put in place, including Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable.

Children

Our service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes affecting your rights will be communicated by email to the address on your order.

Contact

Questions or requests about this policy or your data: markrwilkinsjr@gmail.com
